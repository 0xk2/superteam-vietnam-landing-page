Frequently asked questions

What is Build Station: Winter Challenge?+

A seven-week Solana build program run by Superteam Vietnam, from Dec 1, 2026 to Jan 16, 2027. It ends with an in-person Demo Day and a $10,000 prize pool.

Who can join?+

Students, developers, and developers already building on Solana. Builders from other chains can join too.

Can I join online?+

Yes. The kickoff call, workshops and office hours run online. Build Stations, co-working days and Demo Day happen in person.

Where can I learn Solana before kickoff?+

Start with the Superteam Vietnam Education Hub. It takes you from your first line of Solana code to shipping and fundraising, with resources in English and Vietnamese.

How do I register?+

Fill in the registration form, then subscribe to our Luma calendar to get each session as it opens.

Who counts as a university team?+

A team where every member is enrolled at a university or college in Vietnam. The two top-scoring finalist teams that qualify win Best University Team.

How does this relate to Colosseum?+

Colosseum runs Solana's global hackathons. Winter Challenge is a separate Superteam Vietnam program, and a warm-up for Colosseum 2027.
